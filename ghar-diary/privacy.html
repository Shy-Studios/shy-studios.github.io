Privacy Policy: Ghar Diary – Maid Salary App

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · App ID: com.shystudios.ghardiary · Developer: Shy Studios

In short: Ghar Diary has no login and no server. Everything you type stays on your phone. We (the developer) never see your helpers' names, salaries, leaves or advances. Free users see ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device data for those ads, as explained below.

1. What the app stores, and where

  • Helpers you add (name, role, optional phone number, joining date, notes), their salary settings, leave days, advances, payments and your "name on payslips".
  • All of it is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or to any server we run. We have no server.
  • The app does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS or files.

2. Backups

  • Android Auto Backup: if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. This is done by Google under your Google account settings; we cannot see it.
  • Backup file: Settings → Backup saves a file where you choose (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp). The file contains your records in plain text. Keep it private.

3. Sharing payslips

When you tap Send on WhatsApp, Image or PDF, the app creates the payslip on your phone and hands it to the app you pick. What happens next is up to you and that app. The payslip text may include a link to Ghar Diary on Google Play.

4. Ads (free version)

The free version shows a small banner (not during your first 10 days of use) and, only if you choose it, a short rewarded ad: for example to get a PDF payslip, add more than 3 helpers or export to Excel. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • app interactions with ads (views, taps) and diagnostics (crash and performance data).

This data is sent encrypted. It is not linked to the records you keep in Ghar Diary. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area or the UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ghar Diary Pro users see no ads, and the ads SDK is not started for them.

5. Purchases

If Ghar Diary Pro is offered, it is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro was bought on your Google account.

6. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not create accounts or ask you to sign in.
  • Ghar Diary is a record-keeping tool. It does not lend money, give credit or move money. "Advance" means money you already gave your helper, written down for your own records.

7. Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your phone, you control it: delete a helper inside the app, or uninstall the app (or Settings → Apps → Ghar Diary → Storage → Clear data) to delete everything. Delete any backup files you saved yourself. There is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Children

Ghar Diary is meant for adults who employ household help. It is not directed at children.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Questions: shystudios-support@googlegroups.com
